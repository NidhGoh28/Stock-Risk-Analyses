The Math Behind It

dS = μS dt + σS dW → S(t) = S₀ · exp((μ − σ²/2)t + σ√t · Z)

Where Z ~ N(0,1) is a standard normal random variable. Each path is one possible future. The distribution of final prices gives us the probability of different outcomes.

The Math Behind It

Sharpe = (Rp − Rf) / σp  ·  Max Sharpe ↔ Optimal Portfolio

Modern Portfolio Theory (Markowitz, 1952): for any level of risk, there exists a portfolio that maximises expected return. The frontier traces all such optimal portfolios.
